Quick heads-up: task results and penalties are all in the same `history` list in localStorage. A failed task and a penalty both have `result: 'fail'`, so check `isDistractionPenalty`, `isStarHourPenalty`, and `isStarDailyPenalty` before calling a row a failed task.

Hourly and daily penalties usually mean waiting around for the right time. The console snippet in `how_to_judge_completion.txt` makes one of each for you, and also adds an old entry with no date or work-time fields.

Please leave the `kartavya-main-data` key and saved fields as they are. Reset Score clears the history but keeps tracked work minutes. The MacroDroid URL is blank in this copy, so loading the app won’t ping a live trigger.
